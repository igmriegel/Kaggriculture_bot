# Roadmap

1. **Contract and evidence foundation:** normalized immutable official state and
   complete actions (including hands), fixture capture, safe fallback records,
   and full-horizon evidence matrix.
2. **V2 crop-and-shed economics:** crop catalog, same-day watering and loss
   obligations, fertilizer/yield planning, weed removal, route/shed logistics,
   reserve-aware purchases, bounded market sales, and final liquidation.
3. **V3 hands and land:** ordered hand planner, Fibonacci-cost hiring test, and
   reserve/headroom-safe quadrant expansion.
4. **V4 animals and dynamic market:** structures, animal care/feed/production,
   fertilizer collection, town-demand forecasting, price/glut-aware sales, and
   closing-season liquidation.
5. **V5 operational risk management and active safeguards:** drought weed prevention
   (`consecutive_unwatered` escalation), emergency decay harvesting (`max_lifespan_step` horizon),
   dynamic opening liquidity buffer, and total herd feed retention.

An engine advances only when the same fixed scenarios and seed matrix complete
with zero unhandled errors and unsupported actions, then outperform the prior
promoted engine on that same evidence set.
